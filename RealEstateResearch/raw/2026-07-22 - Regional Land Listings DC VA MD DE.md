## 2. Virginia (VA)
Virginia offers high-volume inventory ranging from budget coastal lots on the Eastern Shore to mountain residential lots in the Shenandoah Valley.

* **Greenbackville & Horntown (Accomack County - Eastern Shore VA)**
  * **Price Range**: $20,000 – $29,900
  * **Lot Size**: 0.25 to 0.50 Acres
  * **Subdivisions**: Captain's Cove & Blue Heron Drive
  * **Utilities**: Community water & sewer available (tap fees apply) or private septic. Community pool, golf course, and boat ramp access near Chincoteague Island.
  * **Play**: Vacation home, spec build, or cheap holding land.

* **Quicksburg & Luray (Shenandoah County - Shenandoah Valley VA)**
  * **Price Range**: $20,000 – $25,000
  * **Lot Size**: 0.50 to 0.65 Acres
  * **Utilities**: Electric at road; private well & perc test required. Mountain views near Shenandoah National Park.

* **Tazewell & Surry Counties (Rural VA)**
  * **Price Range**: $15,000 – $35,000
  * **Lot Size**: 1.0 to 5.0+ Acres
  * **Utilities**: Off-grid / rural well & septic.

## 4. Delaware (DE)
Delaware land under $50,000 is scarce due to high coastal and agricultural demand, but key infill opportunities exist in Sussex and Kent Counties.

* **Seaford & Bridgeville (Sussex County - Delmarva Inland)**
  * **Price Range**: $25,000 – $49,900
  * **Lot Size**: 0.25 to 0.75 Acres
  * **Utilities**: Town sewer/water available in select infill lots (tap fees apply) or private well/septic required. High demand due to proximity to Delaware beaches (Rehoboth/Lewes) and low property taxes.

* **Dover & Harrington (Kent County - Central DE)**
  * **Price Range**: $30,000 – $45,000
  * **Lot Size**: 0.30 to 0.60 Acres
  * **Utilities**: Electric at road; municipal connection or site perc evaluation required.
