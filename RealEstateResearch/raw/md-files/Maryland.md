# Maryland — Tax Lien / OTC / Parcel Research Directory

**Updated:** 2026-09-16

Maryland is a **tax-lien certificate** state. Each county/Baltimore City runs its own tax sale. County-held certificates may sometimes be assigned or sold OTC after the main sale; availability and procedure vary.

## Statewide starting points

- Maryland SDAT Tax Sale Information: https://dat.maryland.gov/Pages/tax-sale-information.aspx
- 2026 Tax Sale Schedule: https://dat.maryland.gov/pages/tax-sale-schedule.aspx
- SDAT Real Property Search: https://sdat.dat.maryland.gov/RealProperty/Pages/default.aspx
- Maryland Land Records: https://mdlandrec.net/
- Maryland iMAP: https://imap.maryland.gov/

### OTC terms to search

Search the jurisdiction for: **county-held certificates**, **assignment sale**, **over-the-counter**, **unsold tax liens**, **leftover certificates**, **county owned liens**, and **assignment list**.

## Suggested Maryland workflow

1. Confirm the certificate is still available with the county.
2. Match the account/parcel in SDAT.
3. Verify parcel shape, acreage, public-road access and improvements in GIS/iMAP.
4. Search Maryland Land Records for the deed, mortgages and releases.
5. Check FEMA flood maps, wetlands, zoning, utilities/septic and HOA/common-area status.
6. Search active and sold listings by exact address/APN.
7. Calculate total basis: certificate + later taxes + legal/title/service + recording/deed costs + any required sale balance.
