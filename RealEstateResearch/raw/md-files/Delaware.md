# Delaware — Tax Deed / Monition / Parcel Research Directory

**Updated:** 2026-09-16

Delaware is **not a conventional tax-lien-certificate state**. Delinquent county taxes can proceed through **monition / sheriff sale**, so research these as property/deed auctions.

## Statewide tools

- Delaware CourtConnect: https://courtconnect.courts.delaware.gov/
- Delaware FirstMap GIS: https://firstmap.delaware.gov/

## Suggested Delaware workflow

1. Check the county sheriff/monition calendar.
2. Search the parcel in the county property database and FirstMap.
3. Search CourtConnect for the monition/sheriff case.
4. Check Recorder of Deeds records.
5. Verify zoning, access, flood/wetlands and improvements.
6. Read the sale terms and determine what interests may survive before bidding.
