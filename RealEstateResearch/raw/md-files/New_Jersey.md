# New Jersey — Tax Lien / Municipal Parcel Research Directory

**Updated:** 2026-09-16

New Jersey sells **tax sale certificates**, but sales are normally run by the **municipality**, not the county. Use each county below as an index to its municipalities, then open the municipality's Tax Collector / Tax Sale / Current Legal Notices page.

## Statewide starting points

- NJ Municipality Search: https://www.nj.gov/nj/gov/direct/municipality.shtml
- NJ County & Municipal Website Directory: https://nj.gov/nj/gov/county/localgov.shtml
- NJ County Directory: https://nj.gov/nj/gov/county/counties.shtml
- NJGIN Property Explorer / parcel data: https://www.nj.gov/njgin/edata/parcels/

## Leftover / municipal-held certificate terms

Ask each municipal Tax Collector for **municipality-held tax sale certificates**, **assignment of municipal certificates**, **unsold tax sale certificates**, and **current legal notices / electronic tax lien sale**.

## Active listing / market-comparison sites

Use the exact parcel address and APN/parcel number where possible.

> An active asking price is not proof of market value. Verify sold comps, zoning, road access, utilities, flood/wetlands, title, parcel geometry, and improvement condition before buying.

## Suggested New Jersey workflow

1. Filter municipalities by county.
2. Open each municipality's Tax Collector, Tax Sale and Current Legal Notices pages.
3. Verify block/lot in NJGIN Property Explorer.
4. Use the applicable county clerk/recording office for deeds and recorded instruments.
5. Check municipal zoning, flood/wetlands, utilities and access.
6. Compare active and sold listings by exact address/block/lot.
