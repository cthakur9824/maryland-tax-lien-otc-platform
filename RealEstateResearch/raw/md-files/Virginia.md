# Virginia — Delinquent Tax Deed / Parcel Research Directory

**Updated:** 2026-09-16

Virginia generally does **not sell tax-lien certificates to investors**. Localities enforce delinquent real-estate taxes through judicial or qualifying nonjudicial **sales of the real property**.

## Statewide starting points

- Virginia Local Government Directory: https://www.virginia.gov/local-government/
- Virginia Code § 58.1-3965: https://law.lis.virginia.gov/vacode/title58.1/chapter30/section58.1-3965/
- Virginia Code § 58.1-3975: https://law.lis.virginia.gov/vacode/title58.1/chapter35.1/section58.1-3975/
- Virginia Circuit Courts directory: https://www.vacourts.gov/courts/circuit.html

## Suggested Virginia workflow

1. Open the locality from Virginia.gov and find Treasurer/Finance delinquent-real-estate sales.
