# Additional U.S. Tax-Lien State Research Files

Updated: 2026-09-16

These files complement the previously created Maryland, New Jersey, Virginia and Delaware notes. This package is focused on states with investor tax-lien certificates, certificates of delinquency, tax-lien assignments, or closely related purchaser lien systems. It intentionally does not treat ordinary tax-deed-only states as tax-lien states.

## Included states

- [[Alabama]] — County tax lien auction/sale is optional by county; Alabama also maintains state-held delinquent-property inventory and certificate assignments.
- [[Arizona]] — Annual county Treasurer tax-lien certificate sales, generally in February. Bid method and online platform vary by county.
- [[Colorado]] — County Treasurer tax-lien sales. Certificates can lead to Treasurer's deed procedures after statutory requirements; research each county's sale rules.
- [[Florida]] — County Tax Collectors sell tax certificates; unsold certificates can become county-held. Tax deed application is a later process.
- [[Illinois]] — County annual tax sales transfer a property-tax lien/tax certificate. Illinois also has county trustee/forfeited tax processes.
- [[Indiana]] — County tax sales issue tax sale certificates. Many counties use third-party auction administrators; county Treasurer/Auditor pages control current rules.
- [[Iowa]] — County Treasurers conduct annual tax sales and issue tax sale certificates. Check each Treasurer for registration, bidding, redemption and assignment rules.
- [[Kentucky]] — County Clerks sell Certificates of Delinquency to registered third-party purchasers. Prior-year holders can have statutory purchase priority.
- [[Mississippi]] — County tax collectors sell tax liens; owners generally have a two-year redemption period. Confirm local online-sale platform and chancery-clerk procedures.
- [[Missouri]] — Most counties use Chapter 140 delinquent-tax certificate-of-purchase sales; some charter/large-city jurisdictions use different foreclosure frameworks. Verify locality.
- [[Nebraska]] — County Treasurers conduct tax sales and issue tax sale certificates under Nebraska law. Procedures and available lists are county-specific.
- [[Ohio]] — Ohio law permits tax lien certificate sales, but many programs are negotiated/bulk sales rather than retail single-parcel auctions. Verify Treasurer policy before researching parcels.
- [[West_Virginia]] — The State Auditor administers delinquent and nonentered land sales and purchaser tax-lien workflows. Use the statewide Auditor system plus county assessor maps.
- [[Wyoming]] — County Treasurers conduct delinquent-property tax sales/certificate processes. Confirm current county sale format and certificate/deed timeline.

## Important classifications

- **Ohio:** tax-lien certificates exist, but county programs may be bulk/negotiated rather than retail parcel-by-parcel.
- **Alabama:** county tax officials may choose lien-auction/sale methods, and the state also maintains state-held delinquent-property inventory.
- **West Virginia:** the State Auditor has a major statewide role in delinquent/nonentered land sales and purchaser lien administration.
- **Missouri:** procedures can differ in certain charter/large-city jurisdictions; always verify the specific locality.
- **Kentucky:** the instrument is commonly a **Certificate of Delinquency**, purchased through the County Clerk by third-party purchasers.
- **Florida:** tax certificates and later tax-deed applications are separate stages.
