# South Florida Multifamily Q2 2026 Report

**Date**: July 2026
**Author/Publisher**: Sunrise Real Estate Analytics
**Target Market**: South Florida (Miami, Fort Lauderdale, West Palm Beach)

## Executive Summary
The South Florida multifamily sector continues to exhibit robust demand driven by population inflows, despite elevated interest rates. Cap rates have seen slight compression this quarter due to intense competition among institutional buyers.

## Key Metrics
* **Miami Multifamily Vacancy**: 4.2% (down 10 bps QoQ)
* **Miami Average Rent**: $2,550 / month (+3.1% YoY)
* **Miami Cap Rates**: 4.75% average for Class A assets
* **Fort Lauderdale Vacancy**: 4.8% (flat QoQ)
* **Fort Lauderdale Cap Rates**: 5.10% average
* **West Palm Beach Vacancy**: 5.0% (up 20 bps QoQ due to new deliveries)
* **West Palm Beach Cap Rates**: 5.25% average

## Major Activity & Players
* **Related Group** has broken ground on a new 350-unit luxury multifamily building in Brickell, Miami. Project completion is scheduled for late 2028.
* **Lennar** completed a 280-unit suburban development in West Palm Beach, contributing to the slight spike in local vacancy metrics.
* **Blackstone** acquired a 400-unit Class A portfolio across Broward County for $180M, signaling continued institutional appetite.
