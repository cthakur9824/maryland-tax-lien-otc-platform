# West Palm Beach Market

West Palm Beach is experiencing a period of supply growth, leading to short-term upward pressure on vacancy rates.

## Market Metrics (Q2 2026)
* **Vacancy Rate**: 5.0% (+20 bps QoQ)
* **Average Cap Rate**: 5.25%

## Activity & Deliveries
* **New Construction**: [[Lennar]] completed a 280-unit suburban multifamily development, which drove the recent increase in local vacancy metrics as the market absorbs new units.
