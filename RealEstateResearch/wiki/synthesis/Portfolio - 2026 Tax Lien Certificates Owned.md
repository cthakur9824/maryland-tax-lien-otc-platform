## 📊 Surplus Paid & Break-Even Recovery Schedule (All 16 Certificates)

Under Colorado law (`C.R.S. § 39-12-103`), **`13.0%/yr` (`1.0833%/mo`)** simple interest accrues **only on the Live Face Amount** (whole-month rounding: any partial month counts as a full month). Every dollar paid in **Surplus (Overbid Premium)** earns **`0%`** interest and is **never refunded** on redemption.

* **Exact Break-Even Formula:** `Break-Even Month = ceil(Surplus Paid ÷ Monthly Interest)`. Every month held *after* the break-even month generates pure profit (`13%/yr` on Face).
* **Hard 36-Month Math Rule (`+13%` Rule):** Every **`+13.0%`** of surplus above Live Face consumes **1 full year (12 months)** of interest. Any surplus exceeding **`39.0%`** of Live Face (`3 × 13%`) **cannot** be recovered from base-lien interest alone inside the 36-month redemption period.
