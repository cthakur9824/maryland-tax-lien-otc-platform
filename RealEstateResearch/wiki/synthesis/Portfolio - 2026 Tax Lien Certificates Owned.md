## 📅 Post-Purchase Servicing & Statutory Lifecycle (Colorado)

1. **ACH Settlement & Certificate Custody (`Nov 2026`)**:
   * Confirm ACH withdrawal clears for Gunnison, Gilpin, and Conejos via **SRI Support (`1-800-800-9588`)** referencing **`TSB1377784`**.
   * Store county-issued **Certificates of Purchase (COP)** or electronic registry confirmations once mailed/emailed by each Treasurer.
2. **Annual Subsequent Taxes ("Sub-Taxes", `Summer 2027 & 2028`)**:
   * Each year after taxes become delinquent (~August), certificate holders have the priority right to pay and endorse **subsequent years' taxes** onto existing certificates to earn the same **`13.0%`** statutory rate and prevent another investor from buying a competing lien.
3. **Redemption Payouts (`Anytime Day 1 – Year 3`)**:
   * Under **`C.R.S. § 39-12-103(6)`**, any partial month rounds up to a full month of simple interest (`13%/yr` on base principal + sub-taxes; initial bonus premiums earn `0%` and are non-refundable).
   * County Treasurers mail redemption checks and year-end **Form 1099-INT** to the buyer address on file under **`TSB1377784`**.
4. **Treasurer's Deed Public Option Auction (`Eligible Nov 17, 2029 – Nov 17, 2041`)**:
   * Under Colorado **`HB24-1056` (`C.R.S. § 39-11.5-101 et seq.`)**, unredeemed certificates at Year 3+ initiate a **Public Auction for a Certificate of Option for Treasurer's Deed** (remote application via email/certified mail + county deposit — e.g., **\$1,000** in Gunnison).
   * If outbid above minimum payoff, you receive 100% principal + accrued `13%` interest + reimbursed costs in cash (or may exercise your statutory option to match). If uncontested, you receive the Treasurer's Deed and clear title via [[Tax Title Clearance and Quiet Title Actions]] (Quiet Title or TTS).
