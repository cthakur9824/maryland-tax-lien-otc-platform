## 💡 Site Feasibility & Development Analysis

### 1. Water & Sewage Connection
* **Public Utility Advantage**: Unlike many 0.5+ acre lots in York County that require 100% septic reserve drainfields, 713 Ship Point Rd is served by county public gravity sewer. Sewer tap connection permits can be secured through York County Environmental Services.
* **Cost Efficiency**: Avoiding engineered septic systems saves $25,000–$40,000 in upfront installation and long-term maintenance costs.

### 2. Community & Zoning Context (Ship Point)
* **Neighborhood**: Ship Point is an established, high-value coastal community along the Poquoson River and Chisman Creek known for quiet streets, custom homes, and private marinas.
* **School District**: Zoned for top-rated York County Public Schools (Dare Elementary, Yorktown Middle, York High).
* **Zoning Setbacks**: Standard York County residential setbacks apply (35 ft front, 10 ft side, 20 ft rear), leaving ample room for a 3,000–4,500 sq ft custom home.

### 3. Investment & Resale Valuation
* Existing single-family homes in the Ship Point community regularly sell between **$600,000 and $950,000+**.
* An acquisition cost of $185,000 enables an attractive all-in construction budget with strong equity upon completion.
