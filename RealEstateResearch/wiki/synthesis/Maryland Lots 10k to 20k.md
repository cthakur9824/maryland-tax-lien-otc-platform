# Maryland Lots 10k to 20k

An investment, feasibility, and deal-sourcing report on vacant land parcels in Maryland priced strictly between **$10,000 and $20,000** (excluding Baltimore City). This synthesis evaluates buildability, perc status, utility access, owner-financing feasibility, and flipping strategies.

---

## Executive Summary & Target Price Bracket

Land priced between **$10,000 and $20,000** represents the prime entry-level range for land investors, homesteaders, and speculative flippers in Maryland. Properties in this bracket fall into three distinct categories:
1. **Utility-Ready Small Infill Lots** (e.g., Kent County public sewer/water lots near $20k).
2. **Mountain & Vacation Subdivision Lots** (e.g., Garrett County Youghiogheny Mountain Resort lots from $16k–$18k).
3. **Large-Acreage Remote / Off-Grid Tracts & Stale Perc Lots** (e.g., Allegany 36-acre mountain tracts at $15k or Somerset 2.5-acre lots at $15k).

> [!TIP]
> **Key Rule for $10k–$20k Land**: Always verify whether a sub-$20k lot is a **buildable parcel** or an unbuildable **"membership lot" / stale-perc parcel**. As detailed in [[Land Search Platforms and Deal Sourcing]], checking 5+ sourcing platforms (including LandWatch, LandSearch, and off-market FSBO tools) ensures you catch deals before retail pricing markups.
