## 💡 Site Feasibility & Development Analysis

### 1. Dual Utility Infrastructure Readiness
* **Municipal Connectivity**: The parcel fronts Yorktown Road with immediate access to municipal water lines and gravity sewer collection lines. 
* **Soil & Perc Redundancy**: In addition to municipal sewer access, preliminary soil perc studies were performed, confirming favorable soil drainage conditions across the tract.

### 2. Subdivision & Yield Potential (R-1 Zoning)
* **Minimum Lot Size**: Standard R-1 zoning in Poquoson requires a minimum lot area of 20,000 sq ft (approx. 0.46 acres) with 100 ft minimum frontage.
* **Potential Lot Yield**: With 5.27 acres and proper cul-de-sac engineering, the tract could potentially yield **4 to 8 high-end single-family residential building lots**, subject to preliminary and final plat approval from the Poquoson Planning Commission.
* **Finished Lot Value**: Individual 0.5-acre buildable lots in Poquoson routinely command **$160,000 to $225,000+** each. A 6-lot yield would generate **$960,000–$1,350,000 in gross lot value**.

### 3. Schools & Demographics
* Poquoson City Public Schools consistently rank among the top 3 school divisions across the entire Commonwealth of Virginia, driving sustained buyer demand and low market times for newly constructed homes ($700,000–$1,200,000+).
