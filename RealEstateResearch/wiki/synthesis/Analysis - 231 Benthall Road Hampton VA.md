## 💡 Site Feasibility & Development Analysis

### 1. Utility Hookup Readiness
* **Municipal Sewer & Water**: Because the parcel is served by City of Hampton municipal gravity sewer and public water, there are no soil perc testing uncertainties or septic reserve drainfield restrictions. Standard water meter and sewer tie-in permits are pulled directly with Hampton Public Works.

### 2. Coastal & Waterfront Permitting (Chesapeake Bay Act)
* **Resource Protection Area (RPA)**: Under Virginia’s Chesapeake Bay Preservation Act, a 100-foot RPA buffer is measured landward from the mean high water mark.
* **Building Envelope**: With 1.34 acres of depth, there is substantial upland area outside the 100-ft RPA buffer to construct a large single-family luxury home without requiring major CBPA variances.
* **Pier & Boat Dock Potential**: The site supports a private pier, boat lift, and floating dock, subject to standard joint permit applications (VMRC, Army Corps of Engineers, and Hampton Wetlands Board).

### 3. Investment & Resale Valuation
* Finished custom waterfront homes along Benthall Road and the Salt Ponds community with private boat docks command **$850,000 to $1,400,000+**.
* Land acquisition at $250,000 with pre-existing municipal utilities offers strong financial feasibility for custom homebuilders or luxury owner-builders.
