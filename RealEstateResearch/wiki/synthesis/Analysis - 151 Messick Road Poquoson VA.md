## 💡 Site Feasibility & Development Analysis

### 1. Pricing Discrepancy & Utility Access
* **Unprecedented Price Basis**: At $125,000 for over 5 acres in Poquoson, the property is priced substantially below typical Poquoson residential acreage ($80,000–$150,000/acre).
* **Municipal Infrastructure**: Public water and sewer are available along Messick Road, enabling straightforward utility connection without the cost and risk of septic mound installation.

### 2. Environmental & Wetlands Dynamics
* **Tidal Creek & Wetlands**: Because the property borders a tidal tributary, a significant portion of the 5.15 acres consists of tidal and non-tidal wetlands and the 100-foot Resource Protection Area (RPA) buffer.
* **Upland Building Pad**: The critical due diligence step is confirming the exact size and boundary of the upland buildable envelope along the front of the parcel. Even with a 0.5-acre to 1.0-acre buildable upland footprint, the remaining acreage serves as an expansive private tidal conservation buffer with kayak/boat access.

### 3. Investment Strategy
* An investor or homeowner can secure 5+ acres of coastal Virginia land with city utilities in a top-rated school district for just $125,000, creating an unmatched low-basis coastal retreat.
