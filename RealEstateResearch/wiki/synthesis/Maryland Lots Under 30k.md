## Developer / Flipping Feasibility Analysis

### 1. General Principles for Cheap Lots (<$30k)
* **National vs. Local Builders**: Large production homebuilders (e.g., Lennar, Ryan Homes, D.R. Horton) **will not buy these lots**. They operate on economies of scale and only buy raw land to develop entire subdivisions (10+ homesites).
* **Target Buyer Pool**: Your target buyer pool for these cheap lots consists of **local custom homebuilders**, **spec home builders**, **modular/mobile home installers**, or **retail end-users** (consumers wanting to build a custom primary or vacation home).
* **Entitlement Margin**: The key to making money on these lots is **risk removal (entitlement)**. Land without active perc approvals or utility verification is a gamble. Land that is "permit-ready" commands a high premium.
