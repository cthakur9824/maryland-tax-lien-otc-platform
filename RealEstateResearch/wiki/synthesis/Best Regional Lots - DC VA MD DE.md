# Best Regional Lots - DC VA MD DE

An executive comparison and deal catalog of the top vacant land opportunities across Washington D.C., Virginia, Maryland, and Delaware. This synthesis evaluates regional market dynamics, utility readiness, perc approval status, zoning density, and investment suitability.

## Regional Investment Overview

The Mid-Atlantic real estate corridor (DC, VA, MD, DE) offers vastly different deal profiles depending on state regulations, coastal demand, and urban density:

```
Regional Land Spectrum
├── Washington D.C.  --> High-Density Urban Infill ($43k–$95k+ | Full Municipal Utilities)
├── Virginia         --> Coastal Resort (Accomack $20k) & Shenandoah Mountain Lots ($20k–$25k)
├── Maryland         --> Utility-Ready Infill (Kent $20k) & Large Mountain Acreage (Allegany $15k / 36 ac)
└── Delaware         --> Coastal Inland Infill (Sussex & Kent Counties $25k–$49k | High Tax Benefit)
```

### 4. Delaware (DE) — Low Tax & Coastal Buffer Lots
* **Seaford & Bridgeville (Sussex County - Inland Coastal DE)**
  * **Price**: $25,000 – $49,900
  * **Size**: 0.25 to 0.75 Acres

   * *Rural Lots (Shenandoah VA, Allegany MD, Somerset MD)*: Ensure purchase agreement includes a **60-day perc test contingency**.
