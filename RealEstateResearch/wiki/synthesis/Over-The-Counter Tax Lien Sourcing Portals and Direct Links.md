# Over-The-Counter Tax Lien Sourcing Portals and Direct Links

A curated operational directory of official platforms, state-level repositories, and county treasurer portals for sourcing and purchasing **Over-The-Counter (OTC)**, **County-Held**, and **Unsold Struck-Off** tax lien certificates and deeds without competing in live auction rooms.

---

## 🌐 1. Major Online Auction Platforms (Hosting OTC & Post-Sale Portals)

These private platforms contract directly with county treasurers to host both competitive annual auctions and post-auction / OTC leftover certificate inventory:

| Platform | Primary States Covered | Direct URL | How OTC / Unsold Is Handled |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **RealAuction (RealTaxLien)** | FL, AZ, NJ, MD, CO | [realauction.com](https://www.realauction.com) | Maintains individual county subdomains (e.g., `countyfl.realtaxlien.com`). Post-auction leftover lists and batch files are downloadable directly from county portal dashboards. |
| **GovEase** | AL, MS, GA, MO, FL, TN | [govease.com](https://www.govease.com) | Hosts online county tax sales and maintains secondary/post-sale catalogs for counties in the Southeast and Midwest. |
| **SRI Services / Zeus Auction** | IN, MI, OH | [sriservices.com](https://sriservices.com) / [sri-taxsale.com](https://sri-taxsale.com) | Primary manager of Indiana **Commissioners' Certificate Sales** (unsold tax lien inventory with accelerated 90–120 day redemption windows). |
| **CivicSource** | LA, TN, MS | [civicsource.com](https://civicsource.com) | Direct public portal for adjudicated tax properties, tax-title deeds, and un-auctioned municipal surplus. |
| **Bid4Assets** | CA, WA, MI, VA | [bid4assets.com](https://www.bid4assets.com) | Coordinates major county re-offer sales and struck-off tax deed auctions with escrow closings. |

## 🏛️ 2. Official Statewide OTC Repositories & Delinquent Land Portals

Certain states operate centralized, state-level departments holding unsold tax certificates and struck-off deeds that can be requested or purchased year-round:

* **Alabama — Department of Revenue (ALDOR)**:
  * **Portal**: [ALDOR Tax Delinquent Property & Land Sales](https://revenue.alabama.gov/property-tax/tax-delinquent-property-and-land-sales/)
  * **Mechanism**: Official statewide "Book of Lands". ALDOR publishes weekly updated county transcripts of tax-delinquent properties held by the state.
  * **How to Buy**: Submit an online price quote application directly through ALDOR. Once quoted, investors have 10 calendar days to remit certified funds. Certificates held < 3 years convey an assignment of certificate; > 3 years convey a tax deed.
* **Arkansas — Commissioner of State Lands (COSL)**:
  * **Portal**: [Arkansas COSL Post-Auction Land Search](https://www.cosl.org)
  * **Mechanism**: Searchable statewide inventory of parcels that failed to sell at annual public land auctions, available for direct OTC purchase.
* **Mississippi — Secretary of State Public Lands**:
  * **Portal**: [MS Secretary of State Public Lands Search](https://www.sos.ms.gov/public-lands)
  * **Mechanism**: Centralized catalog of tax-forfeited state-held real property available for application and direct fee-simple acquisition.
* **West Virginia — State Auditor's Office**:
  * **Portal**: [WV State Auditor Delinquent and Nonentered Lands](https://www.wvsao.gov/)
  * **Mechanism**: Centralized statewide administration of purchaser tax liens earning 12% statutory interest and unredeemed land sales.

## 📍 3. Top County-Level OTC Tax Lien Portals (By Prime Lien States)

### A. Florida (18% Statutory Max — "County-Held Certificates")
In Florida, certificates that receive no bid at the annual May/June auction strike off to the county as **County-Held Certificates** earning the statutory **18% per annum** (1.5% per month):

* **Statewide Directory**: [Florida Association of County Tax Collectors](https://floridataxtreasurers.us/)
* **Market Data & Cross-County Catalog**: [Florida Tax Sales Portal (VisualGov)](https://flataxsales.com)
* **Direct County Portals**:
  * **Palm Beach County**: [pbctax.gov](https://www.pbctax.gov) | Portal: [palmbeachfl.realtaxlien.com](https://palmbeachfl.realtaxlien.com)
  * **Orange County (Orlando)**: [octaxcol.com](https://www.octaxcol.com) | Portal: [orange.realtaxlien.com](https://orange.realtaxlien.com)
  * **Hillsborough County (Tampa)**: [hillstax.org](https://www.hillstax.org)
  * **Pinellas County**: [pinellastaxcollector.gov](https://pinellastaxcollector.gov)
  * **Sarasota County**: [sarasotataxcollector.gov](https://sarasotataxcollector.gov)
  * **Marion County (Ocala)**: [mariontax.com](https://www.mariontax.com)
  * **Polk County**: [polktaxes.com](https://www.polktaxes.com)

### B. Arizona (16% Statutory Max — "OTC Certificate Assignments")
Unsold Arizona liens become available for Over-The-Counter assignment through county treasurer offices or RealAuction portals:

* **Pima County (Tucson)**:
  * **Portal**: [Pima County Treasurer](https://www.to.pima.gov) / [Pima RealAuction](https://pimaaz.realtaxlien.com)
  * **Timing**: OTC "New Assignments" window typically opens every year in **April through mid-December**.
* **Maricopa County (Phoenix)**:
  * **Portal**: [Maricopa County Treasurer](https://treasurer.maricopa.gov)
  * **Process**: Maricopa does not operate an instant e-commerce shopping cart for standard liens; previously offered / unsold tax-deeded parcels must be acquired by submitting a formal **Parcel Offer Form** directly to the Treasurer's Office at 301 W. Jefferson, Phoenix AZ.
* **Cochise County**: [Cochise County Treasurer & Board of Supervisors](https://www.cochise.az.gov)
* **Mohave County**: [Mohave County Clerk of the Board](https://www.mohave.gov) (OTC tax deed offers accepted annually April 15 – November 30).

### C. Maryland (6%–24% Statutory Rate — "Assignment Sales")
Unsold certificates strike off to county commissioners and can be assigned at face value via direct treasury desk requests:

* **Prince George's County**:
  * **Portals**: [PG County Tax Sale](https://taxsale.princegeorgescountymd.gov) & [Tax Inquiry](http://taxinquiry.princegeorgescountymd.gov)
  * **Statutory Rate**: **20.0% statutory interest** on non-principal residential and unimproved land parcels.
  * **How to Order**: Email parcel ID and owner info to `TAXSALE@CO.PG.MD.US`.
* **Baltimore City**:
  * **Portals**: [Baltimore City Finance Bureau](https://finance.baltimorecity.gov/tax-sale) & [Buy Into Bmore Portal](https://buyintobmore.baltimorecity.gov)
  * **Statutory Rate**: **18.0% statutory interest** on residual lien certificates; $1 – $3,000 fixed price for city infill surplus lots.
* **Kent & Anne Arundel Counties**:
  * Evaluated in depth in the vault: [[Deal Analysis - Kent County Tax Liens 2026]] and [[Deal Analysis - Anne Arundel County Tax Liens 2026]].

## ⚠️ Crucial OTC Due Diligence Checklist Before Purchasing

1. **Junk Land Elimination**: Up to 70% of OTC lists are "junk parcels" (substandard easements, retention basins, 1-foot strips, landlocked slivers). Verify lot size (> 2,500 sq ft) and road frontage (> 30 ft) via county GIS.
2. **Surviving Liens & Utilities**: Check for municipal utility liens (e.g., city water/sewer), demolition orders, or EPA environmental liens that do not extinguish.
3. **Bankruptcy Stays**: Cross-reference parcel owners against federal bankruptcy dockets via [PACER](https://pacer.uscourts.gov) to prevent voided transactions under 11 U.S.C. § 362 automatic stays.
