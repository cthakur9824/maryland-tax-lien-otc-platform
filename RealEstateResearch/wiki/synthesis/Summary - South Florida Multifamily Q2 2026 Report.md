# Summary - South Florida Multifamily Q2 2026 Report

This page summarizes the Q2 2026 South Florida Multifamily Market Report compiled by Sunrise Real Estate Analytics.

## Key Insights

### Geographic Markets
* **Miami**: Strongest performance with vacancy dropping to 4.2%. Class A cap rates compressed to 4.75%. Related Group is expanding in Brickell. (See [[Miami Market]])
* **Fort Lauderdale**: Stable market conditions, flat vacancy at 4.8%, average cap rates at 5.10%. Blackstone is active here. (See [[Fort Lauderdale Market]])
* **West Palm Beach**: Short-term vacancy increase to 5.0% due to new deliveries from Lennar. Average cap rates at 5.25%. (See [[West Palm Beach Market]])

### Active Players
* **[[Related Group]]**: Breaking ground on a 350-unit luxury building in Brickell.
* **[[Lennar]]**: Completed a 280-unit project in West Palm Beach.
* **[[Blackstone]]**: Acquired a 400-unit portfolio in Broward County for $180M.
