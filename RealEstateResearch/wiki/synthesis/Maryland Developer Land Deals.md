# Maryland Developer Land Deals

This page tracks large acreage parcels and residential lots in Maryland (excluding Baltimore) that possess high entitlement margins or subdivision potential. These deals are categorized by their developer suitability.

## Developer-Grade Listings

### 1. 3555 Mill Green Road, Street, MD 21154
* **Asking Price**: $997,500
* **Size**: 30.28 Acres
* **County**: Harford County
* **Zoning**: Agricultural (AG)
* **Tax Map / Parcel ID**: [03-036577](https://sdat.dat.maryland.gov/realproperty/) (Harford County SDAT)
* **Flipping & Entitlement Play**:
  * **Development Rights**: Conveys with **three (3) development rights**, allowing a flipper/developer to subdivide the 30-acre tract into three separate upscale residential building lots.
  * **Utility Plan**: Requires on-site private wells and individual septic systems. Perc tests must be scheduled during the wet season.
  * **Agricultural Value**: Eligible for the Maryland Agricultural Land Preservation Foundation (MALPF) easement sale if the buyer elects to preserve the agricultural status. 
  * > [!TIP]
    > To avoid the Agricultural Use Transfer Tax at settlement, the buyer must file an "Agricultural Use Letter of Intent" with the Harford County SDAT office.
