## 💡 Site Feasibility & Development Analysis

### 1. Water & Sewage Configuration
* **Water Connection**: The property fronts Shillelagh Road with an active City of Chesapeake water main. The buyer needs to apply for a standard residential water meter connection and pay the municipal tap fee through the Department of Public Utilities.
* **Septic System**: Because the parcel is south of the municipal sewer line boundary, an on-site septic system is required. The seller has completed soil evaluation and perc testing, yielding an approved engineered septic design on record with the Chesapeake Health Department.

### 2. Zoning & Setbacks (R-15S)
* **Minimum Lot Area**: 15,000 sq ft (Property exceeds minimum with 7.4 acres).
* **Minimum Front Setback**: 35 feet from right-of-way.
* **Side / Rear Setbacks**: 10 feet side / 25 feet rear.
* **Building Envelope**: The cleared central area provides ample room for a 4,000+ sq ft executive home, detached garage/workshop, and pool.

### 3. Investment & Resale Potential
* Homes in the immediate Grassfield/Deep Creek school zones built on 5+ acre private tracts trade in the **$750,000 to $1,100,000+** range.
* At a land cost of $290,000 + site prep/well/septic of ~$45,000 + vertical build cost of $400,000–$500,000, total project cost of $735,000–$835,000 provides strong equity margin.
