## 💡 Site Feasibility & Development Analysis

### 1. Water & Sewage Setup
* **Public Water**: Public water infrastructure lines run along Railway Road, allowing standard residential service connection.
* **Perc Certification**: The parcel has completed soil perc evaluation on record with the Peninsula Health District (VDH), identifying approved primary and 100% reserve drainfield absorption areas for a single-family home.

### 2. Privacy & Zoning Dynamics (RC Zoning)
* **Zoning Purpose**: York County's `RC` district protects the rural and natural buffer character of the central peninsula while permitting low-density custom home estates.
* **Site Buffers**: The 3.29-acre size allows a custom home to be positioned deep into the lot with a 100+ ft wooded buffer from the road, providing extreme privacy while maintaining convenient 5-minute access to shopping, dining, and Interstate 64.

### 3. Construction & Resale Economics
* Custom estate homes on 2–4 acre lots in York County sell in the **$650,000 to $900,000+** range.
* An entry land basis of $180,000 leaves substantial margin for clearing, driveway grading, and custom home construction.
