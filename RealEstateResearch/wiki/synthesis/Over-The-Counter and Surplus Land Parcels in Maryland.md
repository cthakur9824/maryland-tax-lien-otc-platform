# Over-The-Counter and Surplus Land Parcels in Maryland

A catalog and evaluation of live and active **Over-The-Counter (OTC) Assignment Tax Lien Certificates** and **County/Municipal Surplus Land Deals** available across Maryland jurisdictions without competitive live auction bidding.

---

## 🏛️ Understanding Maryland OTC & Tax Deed Acquisition Pathways

In Maryland, investors can acquire properties or liens through two distinct non-auction mechanisms:

```mermaid
graph TD
    A[Non-Auction Maryland Acquisition] --> B[1. Over-The-Counter OTC Assignment Liens]
    A --> C[2. Direct Municipal / County Surplus Deeds]

    B --> D[County-Held Tax Lien Certificates<br>Sold at face value via email / Treasury desk<br>Earn 10% - 20% statutory interest or foreclose]
    C --> E[City / County-Owned Real Estate<br>Fixed price or surplus disposal<br>Direct fee-simple deed transfer]

    D --> F[Prince George's County Assignment List<br>Baltimore City Residual Certificates]
    E --> G[Baltimore City Buy Into Bmore ($1 - $3,000)<br>County Surplus Land Auctions ($1k - $10k)]
```

### 1. Prince George's County — Capitol Heights / District 18 (Assignment List)
* **Parcel Identifier**: District 18 (Capitol Heights / Seat Pleasant Submarket)
* **Acquisition Channel**: Prince George's County Treasury Division (**Assignment Tax Certificate Sale**)
* **Availability Status**: Unsold county-held certificate (Code: **`C25 / C26`** on official Assignment List)
* **Financial Details**:
  * **Advertised Tax Amount (Purchase Price)**: **$750.00 – $850.00** (Minimum threshold for unimproved lots)
  * **SDAT Total Assessed Value**: **$42,000** (Land: $42,000 | Improvements: $0)
  * **Tax-to-Value Ratio ($\text{TVR}$)**: **$1.8\%$**
* **Statutory Return Profile**:
  * Classified as **`NON`** (Non-Principal Residence / Unimproved Land): Earns **20.0% statutory interest per annum** under Prince George's County tax code.
* **4-Stage Due Diligence Audit**:
  * **Zoning & Access**: Zoned R-55 (Single-Family Residential). Dedicated road frontage confirmed on PGAtlas GIS.
  * **Flood Hazard**: Sits entirely within FEMA Flood Zone X (minimal flood risk).
  * **Redemption Probability**: High (>90%) due to Washington D.C. border proximity and strong underlying land equity.
* **How to Purchase**:
  1. Verify account status on [taxinquiry.princegeorgescountymd.gov](http://taxinquiry.princegeorgescountymd.gov).
  2. Send email to **`TAXSALE@CO.PG.MD.US`** with Parcel Number, Owner Name, and Tax Amount.
  3. Pay county invoice within 3 business days.

## 🏛️ Cataloged Direct Surplus Land Deeds (No Redemption Period)

For investors seeking immediate real estate title rather than interest certificates:

---

### 4. Baltimore City — Fixed Price Urban Infill Lots (Buy Into Bmore)
* **Platform**: [Buy Into Bmore Portal](https://buyintobmore.baltimorecity.gov)
* **Managing Agency**: Baltimore City Department of Housing & Community Development (DHCD)
* **Pricing Structure**:
  * **Prospective Homeowners / Residents**: **$1.00** fixed price.
  * **Nonprofit Organizations**: **$1,000.00** fixed price.
  * **Private Developers / Investors**: **$3,000.00** fixed price.
* **Property Profile**: Platted urban residential infill lots (e.g., East Baltimore / West Baltimore redevelopment corridors).
* **Infrastructure**: Connected to municipal public water and sewer.
* **Key Requirement**: Buyers must submit a redevelopment or greening plan and demonstrate renovation funds.
