## 📍 Market-by-Market Breakdown & Utility Details

### 1. City of Chesapeake
* **Infrastructure Context**: Northern and central Chesapeake (Deep Creek, Western Branch, Greenbrier) are primarily serviced by municipal water and sewer through the City of Chesapeake Public Utilities. Southern Chesapeake (Hickory, Grassfield, Great Bridge South) relies heavily on private septic systems and well water due to the city's 2035/2050 comprehensive utility master plan restricting sewer extension.
* **Top Opportunities**:
  * **1813 Shillelagh Rd**: Ideal 7.4-acre lot featuring an active successful perc test and city water available at the street frontage. The homesite is cleared and ready for foundation prep.
  * **1836 Mount Pleasant Rd**: 4.75 acres situated in the Hickory school corridor with perc certification for private residential septic.

### 2. City of Hampton
* **Infrastructure Context**: Hampton is an urbanized, coastal municipality where virtually all infill lots connect to City of Hampton public water and Hampton Roads Sanitation District (HRSD) public sewer mains.
* **Top Opportunities**:

### 3. Yorktown / York County
* **Infrastructure Context**: York County features a hybrid infrastructure network. Subdivisions along the Route 17 / George Washington Memorial Hwy corridor and coastal peninsulas (Ship Point, Dare, Seaford) often have public sewer and Newport News Waterworks service. Larger interior parcels utilize Health Department certified perc drainfields.
* **Top Opportunities**:
  * **713 Ship Point Rd**: 0.58-acre turnkey infill lot with public sewer and water at the road. Sewer tap hookup is immediately available without the need for private drainfield maintenance.

## 🔍 Essential Due Diligence & Permitting Checklist

When proceeding on any of these parcels in Virginia:
1. **Health Department Certification Letter**: For septic lots (e.g., Shillelagh Rd, Railway Rd, Mount Pleasant Rd), request the Virginia Department of Health (VDH) septic permit / certification letter specifying bedroom capacity (e.g., 3-bedroom vs. 4-bedroom conventional/alternative system).
2. **Tap & Capacity Fees**: For municipal utility lots (e.g., Benthall Rd, Ship Point Rd, Yorktown Rd), verify municipal connection fees (City of Chesapeake Public Utilities, Newport News Waterworks, HRSD sewer tie-in fees).
3. **Chesapeake Bay Preservation Act (CBPA) & RPA Buffers**: Verify if any portion of coastal parcels (Poquoson, Hampton waterfront, Ship Point) falls within a 100-foot Resource Protection Area (RPA) buffer.
4. **Zoning & Subdivision Entitlements**: Cross-reference with [[Zoning]] standards and [[Land Search Platforms and Deal Sourcing]] guidelines.
