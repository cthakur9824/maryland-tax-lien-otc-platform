## 🛠️ Step-by-Step OTC Purchase Execution Guide

1. **Verify Land Records on MDLandRec.net**:
   * Navigate to `https://mdlandrec.net/` (Free registration).
   * Select County: **Anne Arundel (County Code 02)**.
   * Search by Book (Liber) and Page (Folio) from the table above.
   * Confirm no open federal tax liens (IRS § 7425 requires 120-day redemption post-foreclosure) or municipal demolition citations.
2. **Verify Judiciary Case Search**:
   * Visit `https://casesearch.courts.state.md.us/casesearch/processDisclaimer.jis`.
   * Search owner name to ensure no active Chapter 7/13 federal bankruptcy automatic stay.
3. **Submit Over-The-Counter Assignment Request**:
   * Contact **Anne Arundel County Office of Finance (Tax Sale Division)**:
     * Address: 44 Calvert St, Annapolis, MD 21401
     * Phone: (410) 222-1144 | Email: `taxsale@aacounty.org`
   * Submit the Account Number, List Number, and certified funds (cashier's check or wire) for the exact lien balance.
4. **Certificate Custody & 6-Month Foreclosure Clock**:
   * Receive physical Tax Sale Certificate of Sale.
   * Statutory redemption window is 6 months from the original sale date under MD Tax-Property Art. § 14-833.
   * Issue 60-day and 30-day statutory notice letters to all interested parties (owners, mortgagees, judgment creditors) before filing Bill to Foreclose Right of Redemption in Anne Arundel County Circuit Court.
