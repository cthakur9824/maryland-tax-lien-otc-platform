## 🌾 Agricultural & 35+ Acre Ranch Lands Suitable to Bid to Flip

### Why Colorado Agricultural Assessments Look Artificially Low (C.R.S. § 39-1-103(5)(a))
Under Colorado statute, land classified as **Agricultural** is NOT assessed at retail market value; it is valued using a 10-year capitalized grazing/hay productivity formula (often **\$40–\$400/acre** on the tax roll), even when the **true retail market value** is **\$2,500–\$9,000+ per acre**. Furthermore, parcels of **35+ acres** are exempt from county subdivision approvals (**C.R.S. § 30-28-101(10)(b)**) and qualify for domestic + livestock well permits.
