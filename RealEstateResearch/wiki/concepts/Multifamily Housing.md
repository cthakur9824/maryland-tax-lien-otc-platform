# Multifamily Housing

Multifamily housing (apartment buildings, complexes, townhouses) represents residential properties containing multiple separate housing units. It is generally classified as Class A (luxury/new), Class B (well-maintained older assets), or Class C (workforce/deferred maintenance housing).

## Current Trends
* **South Florida**: Demand remains high due to strong population inflows, though elevated mortgage rates have kept cap rates higher than historical lows. (See [[Summary - South Florida Multifamily Q2 2026 Report]] for a summary of Q2 2026 metrics).
