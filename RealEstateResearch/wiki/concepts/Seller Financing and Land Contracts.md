# Seller Financing and Land Contracts

A guide to monetizing tax-deeded properties and rural acreage using **Contracts for Deed (Land Contracts)**, creating triple-net style passive cash flow without landlord obligations.

---

## 💡 The Land Contract Monetization Model

For low-to-mid value properties ($20,000 to $80,000) or vacant acreage acquired through tax sales, traditional bank financing is generally unavailable. Selling via a **Contract for Deed** converts a non-income-producing parcel into a high-yielding promissory note.

```mermaid
flowchart LR
    A[Acquire Tax Deed for $2,500] --> B[Sell on Land Contract for $35,000]
    B --> C[Collect $3,000 Down Payment<br>100%+ Capital Recaptured on Day 1]
    B --> D[Finance $32,000 Balance<br>10% Interest | 10-Year Amortization]
    D --> E[Receive ~$422/mo Passive Income<br>Total Return: ~$53,600]
    E --> F[Buyer Pays All Property Taxes,<br>Insurance & Maintenance]
```

## 📈 Standard Deal Architecture

| Deal Element | Typical Terms | Strategic Purpose |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Sales Price** | $30,000 – $60,000 | 2x to 5x of initial tax auction purchase cost. |
| **Down Payment** | $2,000 – $5,000 (avg $3,000) | Recovers 100% of the initial auction cash investment immediately. |
| **Interest Rate** | 9.9% – 12.0% | Strong yield reflecting non-bank subprime seller financing. |
| **Amortization Term** | 7 to 15 Years (10 Years standard) | Keeps monthly payments affordable ($350–$550/mo) for buyers. |
| **Title Conveyance** | Deed delivered upon final payoff | Investor retains legal title; buyer holds equitable title. |
| **Property Upkeep** | **Triple-Net (NNN) Structure** | Buyer pays 100% of property taxes, hazard insurance, and repairs. |

## ⚖️ Regulatory Compliance: Dodd-Frank & SAFE Act

When executing seller financing on residential properties, investors must adhere to federal lending rules:

1. **Dodd-Frank Seller-Financing Exclusion**:
   * **1-Property Exclusion**: An individual seller financing **1 residential property per 12 months** does not need a mortgage loan originator (MLO) license, provided they did not construct the home and the loan is fully amortizing.
   * **3-Property Exclusion**: Financing up to **3 residential properties per 12 months** is permitted without an MLO if the seller is not a builder, the loan has a fixed rate or adjustable rate with a 5-year minimum fixed period, and the buyer has documented ability to repay.
2. **Licensed RMLO Utilization**:
   * For higher deal volume, engage a licensed **Residential Mortgage Loan Originator (RMLO)** ($400–$600 per deal) to underwrite the buyer and generate Dodd-Frank compliant TRID disclosures.
3. **Vacant Land Exemption**:
   * Contracts for Deed on **pure vacant land or commercial lots** are generally **exempt** from Dodd-Frank residential borrower protections.
