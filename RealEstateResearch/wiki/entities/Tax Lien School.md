## 🎯 Investment Philosophy & Methodology

Tax Lien School emphasizes low-risk, capital-disciplined real estate entry points designed to protect investor savings while securing high statutory returns or deeply discounted properties.

### Core Strategic Tenets
1. **Strict Capital Guardrails**: Enforces a rule where no single deal risks more than **one-third (33.3%) of available liquid cash** (e.g., a **$333 maximum per deal** limit on a $1,000 liquid budget).
2. **OTC (Over-The-Counter) First Strategy**: Prioritizes leftover, post-auction municipal certificates to eliminate bidding competition and psychological pressure for novice investors.
3. **Statutory Yield over Speculation**: Focuses on mid-Atlantic jurisdictions like Maryland with high redemption rates and established statutory interest, using interest cash flow to compound capital before scaling to judicial foreclosures.
4. **Structured 90-Day Milestones**: Converts real estate theory into actionable 4-phase timelines (Foundation, Research & Recon, Execution, Close & Reinvestment).

## 📋 Standardized Frameworks

### 1. Three-Tier Deal Structure
* **Tier 1: OTC / Leftover Certificates ($200 – $333)** — Focus on urban infill or rural leftover parcels to master administrative mechanics and interest accrual.
* **Tier 2: Competitive Auction Certificates ($500 – $2,000)** — Scaled bidding on moderate assessed value residential lots with preset bid limits.
* **Tier 3: Judicial Foreclosure / Deed Conveyance ($5,000 – $15,000)** — Attorney-backed legal action in Circuit Court to extinguish owner equity of redemption on unredeemed liens after 12–24 months.

### 2. Client Delivery Tracks
* **Path A — Do It Yourself (DIY)**:
  * Self-paced execution using public county portals, SDAT databases, and open delinquent lists.
  * Solo bidding and independent deal due diligence.
* **Path B — TLS Guided Coaching (Recommended by TLS)**:
  * 1-on-1 advisor reviews of all deal candidates prior to bidding or purchasing.
  * Live Zoom parcel research walkthroughs.
  * Expert validation of max bid calculations.
  * Access to TLS community network and proprietary deal flow.
