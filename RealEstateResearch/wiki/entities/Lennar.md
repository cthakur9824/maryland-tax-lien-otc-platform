# Lennar

Lennar Corporation is one of the largest homebuilders in the United States, active in both single-family homes and suburban multifamily developments.

## Activity
* **West Palm Beach Delivery**: In Q2 2026, Lennar completed a 280-unit suburban multifamily development in West Palm Beach, contributing to short-term supply increases. See [[West Palm Beach Market]].
