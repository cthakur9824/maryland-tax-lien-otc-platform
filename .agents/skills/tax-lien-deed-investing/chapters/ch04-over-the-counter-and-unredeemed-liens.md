# Chapter 4: Over-The-Counter (OTC) & Unredeemed Liens

## Core Idea
When liens or deeds fail to sell at public auction, they "strike off" to the county inventory. Investors can purchase these Over-The-Counter (OTC) / Struck-Off / Repository assets directly from the county year-round without auction competition, earning maximum statutory interest or acquiring real estate for exact back taxes and administrative fees.

---

## Frameworks Introduced

- **The OTC Sourcing & Filtration Matrix**:
  - *Definition*: Also known across states as "County Held Liens", "Lands Available for Taxes" (FL), "Struck-Off List" (TX), "Repository List" (PA), or "Scavenger Sale List" (IL).
  - *Why Assets Land on OTC*:
    1. *Category A (Junk - ~70%)*: Slivers, unusable easements, contaminated land, severe structural liabilities rejected by experienced bidders.
    2. *Category B (Hidden Gems - ~30%)*: High-value residential parcels overlooked due to clerical misspellings in the auction catalog, oversized auction catalogs where buyers ran out of capital, or parcels released from temporary bankruptcy stays post-auction.
  - *Filtering Algorithm*:
    - Step 1: Request or scrape the latest OTC / Lands Available spreadsheet from the county treasurer.
    - Step 2: Remove all parcels with assessed land value < $5,000 or lot area < 2,500 sq ft.
    - Step 3: Filter for improved single-family residential (DOR code 0100) or buildable vacant residential lots with road frontage and utilities.
    - Step 4: Verify current delinquency amount against total market value (target: back taxes < 15% of FMV).

- **The "Least Likely to Redeem" Indicator Framework**:
  - *When to use*: When your primary objective is property acquisition (deed) rather than cash yield.
  - *Indicators of High Non-Redemption Probability*:
    1. *Deceased Owner / Probate Inaction*: Owner listed as "Estate of [Name]" or probate records confirm death with no local heirs.
    2. *Out-of-State / Distant Absentee Owners*: Property mailing address is in a distant state with multiple years of uncollected mail.
    3. *Consecutive Delinquency Years*: Parcels with 3+ consecutive years of unpaid taxes on record.
    4. *Corporate Forfeiture / Dissolved Entities*: Ownership held by an LLC or corporation marked as "Involuntarily Dissolved" with the Secretary of State.
    5. *Vacant / Boarded Physical Condition*: Verified via GIS and physical drive-by.

---

## Key Concepts

- **Over-The-Counter (OTC) Sale**: Direct purchase of unsold tax liens or deeds from the county tax department at face value without an auction bidding process.
- **Struck-Off Property**: Real estate that received no minimum bids at a sheriff or tax deed sale and was legally transferred to the county/municipality to hold in trust.
- **Lands Available for Taxes (FL)**: Florida's statutory repository of tax deeds that were not purchased at the clerk's auction, available for immediate purchase at the opening bid amount plus accrued interest.
- **Assignment of Certificate**: The legal transfer of an existing tax lien certificate from the county (or an existing investor) to a new buyer, transferring all accrued statutory interest and redemption rights.
- **Subsequent Tax ("Sub") Opportunity**: As an OTC lien holder, paying the upcoming annual taxes to roll new funds into the certificate at statutory interest rates without competition.

---

## Mental Models

- **"Cherry-Picking the County Bargain Bin"**: OTC lists are 70% discarded junk, but the remaining 30% are high-margin assets acquired at your own pace without the pressure or price run-up of a live auction room.
- **"The Time-Decay Arbitrage"**: Buying OTC liens that are already 2 years into a 3-year redemption window allows the investor to begin foreclosure proceedings almost immediately rather than waiting the full statutory term.

---

## Anti-patterns & Common Pitfalls

- **Blindly Buying OTC Lists in Bulk**: Purchasing 50 OTC liens for $500 each without GIS verification, only to discover you bought 50 unbuildable 1-foot buffer strips with ongoing mowing and code enforcement fines.
- **Failing to Verify Current Lien Balance**: Assuming the published list price includes subsequent years of taxes that accrued while sitting on the shelf.
- **Neglecting Title Searches on Struck-Off Deeds**: Assuming the county warrants clean title on OTC deeds. Most OTC conveyances are Quitclaim or Special Warranty Deeds containing zero title guarantees.

---

## Key Takeaways

1. OTC lists offer year-round, non-competitive acquisition of tax liens and tax deeds directly from county treasurers.
2. Filter OTC lists rigorously to eliminate unbuildable strips, contaminated land, and non-conforming parcels.
3. Look for seasoned OTC certificates that have already accrued multiple years toward their redemption window to shorten the timeline to foreclosure.
4. Target deceased owner estates, multi-year delinquencies, and out-of-state absentee owners when seeking real estate acquisition rather than certificate redemption.
