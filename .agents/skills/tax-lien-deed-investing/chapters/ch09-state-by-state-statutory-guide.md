# Chapter 9: 50-State Statutory Classification & Auction Directory

## Core Idea
Real estate tax foreclosure is strictly a creature of state statute. The United States is divided into four major legal regimes: **Tax Lien States**, **Tax Deed States**, **Redeemable Deed States**, and **Hybrid States**. An investor's returns, risk profile, and time-to-ownership depend entirely on the statutory parameters of the state in which the property sits.
