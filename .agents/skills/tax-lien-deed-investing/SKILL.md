## Core Frameworks & Mental Models

### 1. The Government First-Lien Super-Priority
Property tax liens are statutory super-priority liens. When issued, they sit senior to all private mortgages, deeds of trust, mechanic's liens, and judgment liens. When foreclosed via a tax deed sale, all subordinate private liens and mortgages are extinguished by operation of law (provided constitutional notice standards were met).

### 2. The 3 Statutory Market Regimes
- **Tax Lien States** (e.g., FL, AZ, CO, NJ, MD, IN, IA): You purchase first-position debt certificates earning 8%–36% statutory interest. Over 95% of liens redeem, providing predictable, secured cashflow.
- **Tax Deed States** (e.g., CA, NV, WA, NY, NC, MI, AR): The county forecloses the property and sells physical ownership directly at auction with no post-sale redemption.
- **Redeemable Deed States** (e.g., TX, GA, CT, RI): The investor receives a recorded deed immediately, but the owner has 6–24 months to redeem by paying a mandatory flat penalty (e.g., Texas 25% year 1, 50% year 2; Georgia 20% flat penalty).

### 3. The 4-Stage Due Diligence Funnel
Never bid on an address alone. Follow this strict filtration sequence:
1. **Desktop Filter**: Eliminate assessed values < $5,000; filter for Single Family (DOR 0100) or buildable residential lots; ensure Tax-to-Value Ratio (TVR) < 20%.
2. **GIS & Geospatial**: Verify parcel boundaries, road frontage (> 30 ft), non-flood status (FEMA Zone X), and satellite structural condition.
3. **Encumbrance Check**: Screen for open IRS federal tax liens (120-day redemption rule), municipal demolition/water liens, and federal bankruptcy stays (PACER).
4. **Physical Verification**: Drive-by visual inspection of roof, foundation, and occupancy status; calculate Maximum Allowable Bid (MAO) in ink.

### 4. Bidding Discipline & Auction Mechanics
- **Bid-Down Interest (FL, AZ)**: Bidders drive interest rates down from statutory caps (e.g., 18% to 0.25%). In Florida, the statutory 5% mandatory floor guarantees a minimum 5% penalty return upon redemption regardless of the bid.
- **Premium Bidding (Deed States)**: Bidders bid dollars up. Never exceed pre-calculated MAO:
  $$\text{MAO} = (\text{ARV} \times 0.70) - \text{Rehab} - \text{Title Certification (\$2,500)} - \text{Closing Costs}$$
- **Immediate Settlement Rule (e.g., Harris County, TX)**: Certified funds must be delivered immediately upon winning each parcel.

### 5. Post-Acquisition Monetization Engine
- **Title Clearance**: Raw tax deeds have clouded title. Clear title via **Tax Title Services (TTS)** (30–60 days, ~$1,800) or judicial **Quiet Title Action** (3–6 months, ~$3,500) before selling retail.
- **Contract for Deed (Land Contract)**: Sell low-to-mid value properties ($30k–$90k) on seller financing with $3,000 down, amortized over 10 years at 10% interest. The buyer covers all maintenance, taxes, and insurance.
- **Occupant Resolution**: Use friendly **"Cash for Keys"** ($500–$2,000) before initiating formal court ejectment.
