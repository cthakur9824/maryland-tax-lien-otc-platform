## 2. Instant Disqualification ("Red Flag") Rules

- **Rule 1 (Lot Width)**: If parcel frontage < 25 ft or total area < 2,500 sq ft (urban) / < 0.25 acre (suburban) → **DISQUALIFY** (likely unbuildable sliver/spite strip).
- **Rule 2 (Land-to-Building Ratio)**: If assessed improvement value is $0 on a residential street where neighboring homes are $200k+ → **GIS CHECK** (confirm if vacant lot or burned/demolished structure).
- **Rule 3 (Environmental Hazard)**: If former auto repair, gas station, dry cleaner, or industrial yard → **DISQUALIFY** (strict CERCLA federal cleanup liability attaches to deed holder).
- **Rule 4 (Access)**: If GIS shows no dedicated public road frontage or legal deeded easement → **DISQUALIFY** (landlocked parcel).
- **Rule 5 (FEMA Floodplain)**: If parcel sits 100% inside Flood Zone A / AE / VE without existing elevated foundation → **DISQUALIFY** (uninsurable / non-buildable).

## 3. Bidding Mathematics & Maximum Allowable Bid (MAO)

### For Tax Deed Properties (Targeting Wholesale / Flip):
$$\text{MAO} = (\text{After Repair Value (ARV)} \times 0.70) - \text{Estimated Rehab Costs} - \text{Title Clearance Fees (\$2,500)} - \text{Holding/Closing Costs}$$

### For Redeemable Deed Properties (Texas / Georgia):
$$\text{Max Bid} \le \text{ARV} \times 0.50 \quad \text{(Ensures lucrative margin whether owner redeems at 25\% or defaults to deed)}$$

### For Tax Lien Certificates:
- In **Bid-Down States (FL, AZ)**: Set minimum acceptable rate at **0.25% in Florida** (guaranteed 5% statutory floor) and **6.0% in Arizona**.
- In **Premium Bidding Lien States (CO, IN)**: Total bid amount (base lien + premium) must never exceed **15% of the property's conservative liquidation value**.

## 5. Auction Day Execution Checklist

- [ ] **Catalog Diff**: Download final auction catalog update 1 hour prior to sale; strike off redeemed/pulled parcels.
- [ ] **Certified Funds**: Split cashier's checks into $1k, $5k, $10k increments payable to County Clerk.
- [ ] **Written MAO Cap**: Write your absolute ceiling bid in ink next to every parcel. Never bid a single increment higher.
- [ ] **Pre-Sale PACER Check**: Confirm top 5 targets have no open bankruptcy filings filed that morning.
- [ ] **Post-Sale Security**: If winning a deed, dispatch locksmith and place vacant insurance policy within 24 hours.
